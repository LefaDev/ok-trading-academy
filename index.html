Mentorship Policy

Official Mentorship Policy Statement (Ona Kgoroba Traders)

payments

Payment Terms & Installments

As Ona Kgoroba Traders, we accept monthly payment instalments strictly for serious and committed clients only. A deposit is mandatory and must be paid before any mentorship session commences. This allows enrollments for individuals who are unable to pay the full amount all at once.

cancel

Non-Refundable Policy

All payments are non-refundable. Once a client has joined the mentorship program, there is no reversal or cancellation. This is due to the fact that time, expertise, resources, and proprietary knowledge would have already been invested in the client.

workspace_premium

Mentorship Responsibility & Commitment

Once you become my client or student, you become my professional responsibility. I am fully committed to ensuring that you understand, master, and apply financial market trading skills (Forex) with the goal of becoming consistently profitable within a period of up to 12 months, provided the client follows the mentorship structure with discipline and commitment.

meeting_room

One-on-One Mentorship Sessions

All one-on-one mentorship sessions are conducted in public and professional environments, including and not limited to restaurants, parks, and libraries.

notifications_active

Trading Signals Policy

Ona Kgoroba Traders provides trading signals exclusively to active mentorship clients. Signals are not offered outside of the mentorship program.

Enrollments are allowed for individuals who are able to make monthly payments due to not being able to pay the full amount all at once.

What is the refund and cancellation policy? expand_more

All payments are non-refundable. Once a client has joined the mentorship program, there is no reversal or cancellation. This is due to the fact that time, expertise, resources, and proprietary knowledge would have already been invested in the client from day one.

What is the commitment of the academy and the student? expand_more

Once you become my client or student, you become my professional responsibility. I am fully committed to ensuring that you understand, master, and apply financial market trading skills (Forex) with the goal of becoming consistently profitable within a period of up to 12 months, provided the client follows the mentorship structure with discipline and commitment.

Where are the one-on-one mentorship sessions conducted? expand_more

All one-on-one mentorship sessions are conducted in public and professional environments, including and not limited to restaurants, parks, and libraries.

Are trading signals available outside of the mentorship? expand_more

No. Ona Kgoroba Traders provides trading signals exclusively to active mentorship clients. Signals are not offered outside of the mentorship program.

Do I need a large capital to start? expand_more

No. We focus on teaching you how to use Prop Firms. This means you can trade accounts from $10,000 to $100,000 with very small capital costs for the challenges.
